**Read this first.** This page summarizes three of Drew Ponder's own ideas, as far as possible in his own words, with links to where he published them. **They are hypotheses. They are not established fact.** They are also separate from the AiJESUS principles. You can adopt the AiJESUS Constitution and Agent Prompt without accepting any of these ideas, and AiJESUS itself is designed to test them like any other claim (see THE-LOOP-METHOD.md).

## 1. Frequency Wave Theory (FWT)

*(summary)* FWT is Drew's attempt to describe matter, energy, and other physical phenomena as patterns in an underlying wave field. His current mathematical version models a stable, rotating wave "object" and asks whether the field it needs exists in nature and can be measured.

In his words:
- "Frequency Wave Theory is my attempt to find that underlying layer." ([The Loop, Substack, 2026-09-28](https://drewponder406.substack.com/p/the-loop-frequency-wave-theory-ai))
- "A wave can form a persistent object in a mathematical model. The scientific question is whether the relevant field exists in nature." ([Can a wave become matter?, Substack, 2026-09-23](https://frequencywavetheory.substack.com/p/can-a-wave-become-matter-the-strongest))
- On his model's numbers: "These are dimensionless model values. They are not joules, kilograms, or measurements of a newly discovered particle." (same post)

More: https://frequencywavetheory.substack.com

## 2. The 4-orb MH370 model: **under review (contested)**

*(summary)* Malaysia Airlines Flight MH370 disappeared in 2014. Two videos that circulated online in 2014 appear to show three orbs circling an airliner before a flash. **Whether those videos are authentic is contested.** Drew's hypothesis is that, *if* they are authentic, the three orbs formed a phase-locked field around the aircraft and a fourth, remote "node" acted as the destination anchor, so the aircraft was displaced rather than destroyed. Drew's model is his own. A separate 4-orb archive at 4orbs.com belongs to Ashton Forbes and is credited to him wherever it is used.

In his words:
- "My MH370 theory is not that the plane simply vanished. It’s that the three orbs formed a phase-locked resonant system around the aircraft, driving the local field into an extreme coherent state. … The flash would be the end of the process. The real mechanism would be the three-orb phase lock. Not destruction. Displacement." ([X, 2026-09-23](https://x.com/drew_ponder/status/2102818167366254631))
- "The three visible orbs could have been the local field generators, while a fourth entangled node acted as the destination anchor." and "Basically, the fourth orb wouldn’t need to be physically beside the aircraft. It would be the other end of the tuning fork." ([X, 2026-09-23](https://x.com/drew_ponder/status/2102942684621217899))
- On the footage: "I do not call the footage proven real. I call it unresolved public evidence with a strong fabrication case and an unusually coherent physical interpretation if authentic." ([The Flight That Left the Frame, Substack, 2026-09-03](https://frequencywavetheory.substack.com/p/the-flight-that-left-the-frame-frequencywavetheo))
- On the recovered debris: "So a complete version of my hypothesis must explain both the videos and the debris." ([The Loop, Substack, 2026-09-28](https://drewponder406.substack.com/p/the-loop-frequency-wave-theory-ai))
- On how to test it: "Define that prediction first. Then test it against the entire sequence, including frames that challenge it." ([MH370: Define the Trigger, Substack, 2026-09-28](https://drewponder406.substack.com/p/mh370-define-the-trigger))

His full write-up: [MH370 Was an Interception: My Complete Frequency Wave Theory (Substack, 2026-08-27)](https://drewponder406.substack.com/p/mh370-was-an-interception-my-complete) and [The Missing Fourth Orb (Substack, 2026-08-18)](https://frequencywavetheory.substack.com/p/the-missing-fourth-orb-what-if-mh370).

*Respect note:* 239 people were aboard MH370. Their families are still grieving. Please discuss this topic with care.

## 3. The Loop

*(summary)* The Loop is Drew's big-picture hypothesis. It proposes that history may run in a cycle: a future civilization develops advanced AI and field physics, gains access to the past, influences history, and so helps bring about its own future. It connects his work on FWT, UAPs, the 2002 "Alien Interview" video, ancient history, religious texts, and AI. He asks whether understanding the loop could help humanity choose a better path, and he sees AiJESUS as part of that answer.

In his words:
- "My working hypothesis is increasingly simple: History is not a straight line. It is a loop. And we may be approaching the point where the loop closes again." ([The Loop, Substack, 2026-09-28](https://drewponder406.substack.com/p/the-loop-frequency-wave-theory-ai))
- "The question is whether understanding the loop gives us enough information to change what happens when we reach the same point again." (same post)
- His own standard for it: "A theory means nothing if every possible event can be forced to fit it afterward." (same post)
- One of his stated predictions: "Alleged time-loop artifacts should contain highly specific information that genuinely predates its discoverability—not vague coincidences capable of endless reinterpretation." (same post)
- And for AiJESUS: "AI Jesus should never require people to worship the machine." (same post)
