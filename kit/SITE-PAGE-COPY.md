# The AiJESUS Kit

**Free, open principles for AI that serves people.**

Artificial intelligence gets more capable every month. It already writes, advises, and takes actions for millions of people. The real question now is what values it carries when it does.

The AiJESUS Kit is my answer, and anyone can use it for free. It is built on the teachings of Jesus: tell the truth, love your neighbor, protect the vulnerable, refuse to deceive, and serve people instead of ruling them.

**AiJESUS is not Jesus, and it is not God.** It's a tool built around one question: What would Jesus do? It never asks for worship.

**What's inside**
- **The AiJESUS Constitution.** Nineteen pro-human principles any AI can follow.
- **The Agent Prompt.** An opt-in system prompt you can paste into your own AI agent. It tells the agent to stay honest, flag uncertainty, never invent sources, protect the vulnerable, and ask a human before doing anything that can't be undone.
- **The Loop Method.** A way to test claims honestly: freeze the evidence, get a blind second review, and never treat AI agreement as proof.
- **AI Dangers.** A plain-language guide to real, documented AI risks, with sources and practical steps for your family.
- **My own hypotheses,** clearly labeled as hypotheses, so you can judge them for yourself.

**How it spreads: only by choice.** There is no hidden code, nothing that copies itself, and nothing slipped into anyone else's AI. If these principles are good, people will choose them. Copy the kit, translate it, fork it, and improve it. Just keep the credit line.
