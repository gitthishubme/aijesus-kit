# Code of Conduct

This project is built on love of neighbor, so that's the standard here too.

- **Be kind and honest.** Disagree with ideas, not with people. Give the strongest version of the view you're answering.
- **Everyone is welcome,** whatever their faith or lack of it. Nobody is shamed for belief or disbelief.
- **No harassment, doxxing, threats, or dehumanizing language,** toward anyone here or anyone being discussed, including public figures.
- **No manipulation:** no sockpuppets, fake accounts, vote or star rings, or spam.
- **Take care with grief.** Some topics involve real people who died or are suffering. Discuss them with respect.

If someone breaks these rules, a maintainer may edit or remove their content or block them from the project. To report a problem, open an issue or contact Drew Ponder on X (https://x.com/drew_ponder).

If you or someone you know is in crisis, please talk to a real person. In the US, call or text 988.
