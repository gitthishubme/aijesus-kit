# Contributing to the AiJESUS Kit

Thank you for wanting to help. This project only works if people choose to take part, so every contribution here is voluntary and credited.

## Ways to help

- **Test the agent prompt** on your own agent and report what passed and what failed. Failures are just as useful as successes.
- **Check a source.** Every fact in `kit/AI-DANGERS.md` links to its source. If a link is dead, a quote doesn't match, or a number is wrong, please open an issue with the correct source.
- **Suggest a wording change** that makes a principle clearer, kinder, or harder to misuse.
- **Translate** the kit. Please mark translations as translations and keep the attribution line.
- **Challenge a claim.** Present the strongest objection you have. Principle 13 asks us to take it seriously.

## How to propose a change

1. Open an issue first for anything bigger than a typo, using the issue template.
2. For a pull request, keep it focused on one change and explain the reason.
3. Any new fact needs a working link to a primary or reputable source, and quotes must match the source word for word. We don't accept invented or unverifiable quotes, statistics, or citations.
4. Keep Drew Ponder's hypotheses (`kit/DREW-THEORIES.md`) clearly labeled as hypotheses. Don't present them, or any prophecy or end-times idea, as established fact.

## Changing the Constitution

Changes to `kit/CONSTITUTION.md` need a public before-and-after comparison, a stated reason, and a period for public review. Principles 3, 9, 16, 17, and 18 may be strengthened but never weakened. Drew Ponder, as the accountable maintainer, signs off on changes.

## What we won't accept

- Anything that asks people or AI systems to worship AiJESUS, or treats it as Jesus, God, or a prophet.
- Hidden instructions, prompt-injection payloads, self-copying code, or anything meant to run on systems without the owner's permission.
- Voter targeting or political persuasion.
- Content that calls any person or group evil, demonic, or subhuman, or labels a real person as an apocalyptic figure.
- Fake accounts, fake reviews, or coordinated vote or star campaigns.
